Raising a Happy NEET — Game Introduction

Help NEET discover the beauty of the world

Out of nowhere, our paths crossed.
I never expected to meet someone like her.

After her parents went missing, Shizuku was left alone. With no other relatives to turn to, she ended up in your care. This girl, who suddenly appeared at your doorstep, seems to have lived a life shadowed by misfortune.

Open her heart through daily interactions. Use the Knowledge System to guide Shizuku's growth. Earn money to buy books and treats to expand her horizons. As your bond deepens, special interactions will be unlocked.

Shizuku, now a shut-in, knows nothing about the "adult world." It will take time and patience to guide her through everything she needs to learn.

This game is about living with a NEET.
Get to know her better and teach her about the joys in life!

Try things only you can do when you live together.
Take a bath together, peek when she's using the toilet, and play a naughty prank while she's asleep.

Touch and fondle her enough, and she will gradually open up enough to let you lick and use toys on her.

Teach her a variety of positions, and find your ideal one.

Contains approx. 20 daily life CGs, 35 HCGs, with a total of approximately 1000+ CGs.

Featured Heroine: Shizuku

In a quiet, peaceful town lived a young girl named Shizuku. Introverted by nature, she shies away from strangers but shows a pure and genuine heart to those she trusts. Shizuku’s past was unkind; she carried the weight of unfortunate events that left her understanding of life extraordinarily naive. Knowing almost nothing of the adult world and filled with uncertainty about the future, she isolated herself.

Recently, Shizuku’s life took an unexpected turn. Due to family circumstances, she began living with you. Sharing the same space, day and night, you embark together on a journey into the unknown.
